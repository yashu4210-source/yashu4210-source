
# Hospitality AI Receptionist

## Guest Enquiry Flow

### 1. Welcome
Welcome to our Hospitality Assistant!

### 2. Identify Enquiry
- Hotel Booking
- Adventure Park
- Restaurant
- Spa and Wellness
- Other Services

### 3. Collect Basic Details
- Guest Name: (Optional for demo)
- Enquiry Type:
- Number of Guests:
- Preferred Date:

### 4. Next Action
- Provide available information.
- Ask for missing details.
- Escalate to a human team member when needed.

## Important
This is a learning prototype.
Use fictional guest information only.
No live bookings or payments are supported.
